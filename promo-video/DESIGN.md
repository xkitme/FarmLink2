# FarmLink Promo Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Use the existing Agro-Modernist Tech design language from `design_ref/stitch_farmlink_smart_rural_platform/agro_modernist_tech/DESIGN.md`: a professional, tactile agricultural SaaS aesthetic that connects soil, field work, and cloud intelligence. The film should feel clean, reliable, accessible, and optimistic, with soft off-white surfaces, leaf green hierarchy, earthy brown structure, and golden wheat accents.

## Colors

- Background: `#F9F9F9`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Primary leaf green: `#0D631B`
- Primary container: `#2E7D32`
- Earth brown: `#7A5649`
- Wheat accent: `#FFBA38`
- Text: `#1A1C1C`
- Secondary text: `#40493D`

## Typography

- Primary typeface: Noto Sans, matching the FarmLink app design system.
- Use heavy weights for video-scale headlines and tabular numerals for metrics.

## What Not To Do

- Do not mention offline, local fallback, or single-machine positioning in user-facing promo copy.
- Do not use cold neon, purple-blue tech gradients, or generic dark SaaS styling.
- Do not use heavy black shadows; use soft green or brown-tinted depth.
- Do not introduce colors outside the project palette.
